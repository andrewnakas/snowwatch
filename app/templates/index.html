How the ensemble works

Each member predicts the next 7 days of snow depth, then we anchor every member to today's observed depth and blend them with weights proportional to 1 / rolling_MAE. Members with no skill on the last few weeks of holdout data get near-zero weight.

Members: persistence_lag1 (today's depth held flat), climatology (per-day-of-year mean), snow17 (full Anderson 1976 SNOW-17 conceptual snowpack — temperature-index melt, refreeze, rain-on-snow, density compaction), nbm_snow17 (same physics driven by NBM precip/temperature; absent outside NBM coverage), nbm_snowfall (NOAA NBM forecast snowfall accumulated on the current pack), ridge_snow (LightGBM on lagged depth + temp/precip windows), chronos_bolt (Amazon Chronos zero-shot foundation model).
